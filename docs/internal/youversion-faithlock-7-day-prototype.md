# FaithLock 7-Day Bible + Prayer Prototype

Status: INTERNAL ONLY — draft for product and measurement review
Owner: Dev
Scope: a bounded prototype for a possible FaithLock reading/prayer series; no YouVersion contact, submission, publication, paid API call, Store change, or Production change.

## Product hypothesis

A short, completion-oriented Bible and prayer journey can give a new FaithLock user a calm first week of practice without claiming that a completed day is an installation, activation, purchase, payer, or revenue event. If a future eligible CTA is added, its `faithlock_store_click` receipt remains a web proxy and must not be counted as an installation or revenue. The prototype is intentionally content-only and is not wired to the app or a public route.

## Seven-day series

### Day 1 — Make Space

Scripture: Psalm 46:10

Reflection: Notice the noise before trying to defeat it. A small pause is not failure or wasted time; it is an honest way to become present to God and to the choice in front of you.

Prayer: God, help me become still enough to notice you. Give me grace to begin with one quiet choice rather than demanding a perfect day. Amen.

Practice: Put the phone out of reach for five minutes. Take three slow breaths, name one distraction, and return to the next faithful task.

Completion definition: The reader has opened Day 1, read the reflection, and intentionally completed the five-minute pause. No claim is made about focus quality.

### Day 2 — Ask for Wisdom

Scripture: James 1:5

Reflection: Wisdom is not only a large decision. It can be the next small, truthful action: closing a tab, answering with patience, or asking for help instead of reacting.

Prayer: Father, I need wisdom for the choices in front of me. Make the next right step clear and give me humility to take it. Amen.

Practice: Write one decision that is competing for your attention. Ask for wisdom, then choose one next action that can be completed in ten minutes.

Completion definition: The reader has recorded or named one decision and completed one next action. The action is self-reported and not a business outcome.

### Day 3 — Guard Your Attention

Scripture: Proverbs 4:23

Reflection: Attention is a form of stewardship. Guarding it is not about fear or control; it is about choosing what deserves access to your limited time and love.

Prayer: Lord, teach me to guard my attention without becoming harsh with myself. Help me choose what leads toward love, truth, and faithful work. Amen.

Practice: Choose one distracting app to leave closed for a 15-minute block. If you open it, notice without shame and begin the block again.

Completion definition: The reader has attempted one 15-minute attention block. The prototype must not infer success, retention, or reduced screen time from the attempt.

### Day 4 — Receive Rest

Scripture: Matthew 11:28-30

Reflection: Rest is not a reward for perfect performance. It is a way to remember that worth is not measured by output and that limits are part of being human.

Prayer: Jesus, meet me in my weariness. Teach me a gentle rhythm of work and rest, and keep me from turning discipline into condemnation. Amen.

Practice: Take a screen-free ten-minute rest. Do not fill it with a productivity goal; simply receive the pause.

Completion definition: The reader has completed the ten-minute rest or intentionally stopped early and noted why. No wellness or spiritual effect is asserted.

### Day 5 — Return When Distracted

Scripture: Luke 15:4-7

Reflection: Returning matters more than never wandering. When attention slips, the next faithful moment is still available; begin again without turning the detour into an identity.

Prayer: God, when I wander, help me return with honesty and hope. Let a small return be enough for this moment. Amen.

Practice: When you notice distraction, say, “I can return,” then take one deliberate action toward the task or relationship that matters.

Completion definition: The reader has noticed one distraction and made one deliberate return. This is a self-reported practice, not proof of behavior change.

### Day 6 — Choose Faithful Limits

Scripture: 1 Corinthians 10:23-24

Reflection: Freedom includes the wisdom to decline what is not helpful. A limit can create room for attention, service, and rest without becoming a verdict on anyone who needs a different boundary.

Prayer: Give me discernment to choose limits that serve love. Keep me from comparison, pride, and shame as I learn what helps me be faithful. Amen.

Practice: Set one temporary boundary for the next hour: a device location, a notification pause, or a single-task commitment.

Completion definition: The reader has chosen and attempted one boundary. The contract records the attempt only; it must not report a successful block unless the product later proves it.

### Day 7 — Continue with Grace

Scripture: Philippians 1:6

Reflection: A seven-day practice is a beginning, not a finish line. Keep what was life-giving, release what became burdensome, and choose one small rhythm to carry forward.

Prayer: God, thank you for meeting me in small beginnings. Show me one gentle rhythm to continue, and give me grace when I need to begin again. Amen.

Practice: Write one rhythm to continue for the next seven days and one pressure to release. Share neither by default; this prototype does not collect private journal text.

Completion definition: The reader has selected a next rhythm and completed the series review. A completed series is not an activation, purchase, payer, or revenue event.
