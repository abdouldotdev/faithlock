## Short answer

For scheduled limits on iPhone, compare each app by the time rule it supports, which apps or sites it can cover, and what happens when you try to open a blocked app. There is no universal best: the right choice depends on whether you want a scheduled limit, an active pause before access, or both.

This guide keeps the comparison bounded to what an official product page or App Store listing supports. It does not rank apps by score, price, effectiveness, or install results. ## What a scheduled time blocker should answer

Before choosing an iPhone app, check four separate questions:

- **When does the rule run?** Look for scheduled lock times, selected days, or a recurring session rather than an on-demand block only.
- **What can it cover?** Confirm whether the official source names apps, websites, categories, or another scope.
- **What happens at the boundary?** A schedule and an unlock interaction are different features. One can define when a block starts; the other can add a pause or a required action when access is attempted.
- **What device is supported?** Confirm current iPhone support in the official listing before installing. Do not infer support for another platform from an iPhone listing.
